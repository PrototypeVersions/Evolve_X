Where these ideas came from

Evolve_X brings together and rebuilds the ideas behind two earlier projects, Rousix and the Intermetanet. It keeps the questions they asked and rebuilds the answers so they can work in the real world.

Why rebuild instead of start over

The questions were right. People are working harder for less. The AI economy is concentrating fast. Ordinary families are shut out of owning what is being built, and the financial system often feels designed to keep it that way.

Those questions deserve answers that can survive a lawyer, an accountant and a skeptic, and still be worth getting excited about. That meant keeping the ambition, keeping the people-first instinct, and replacing anything that depended on speculation, secrecy or a single leader.
